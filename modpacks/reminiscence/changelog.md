# Reminiscence 2.0.1

[ CONTENT ]
- Added Blockswapper

      - Longbow; a bow which has the post-beta 1.8 bow drawback mechanics

[ TWEAKS ]
- Elephants, Hippos, & Rhinos have been beta-fied and re-enabled!
- Voidlanders no longer pick up blocks
- Further increased sparsity of Jungle & Mangrove Swamp trees
- Video Settings button now takes you to the vanilla one (Embeddium video settings can still be accessed via mod configs button)
- Ocean Shrines & Ancient Cities can now be found via Cartographer Quills
- Removed Golden Amber; replaced all instances of them with Gold Ingots
- Emeralds are now in more loottables
- Redid biome distribution for 500th time
- Properly configured Crash Assistant to direct users to this discord (Courtesy of Omo50)
- Several trees, such as Mangrove and Deep Aether trees, have been simplified
- Glow Squids no longer apply Glowing debuff on hit
- Reworked Crystal Lamps; their recipe now consists of a redstone lamp and a respective dye
- Temperate Rainforests have been completely replaced with regular Taiga biomes
- Bamboo Jungles & Sparse Jungles have been completely replaced with regular Jungles; bamboo has been made more common to compensate
- Rich Soil can now enchant into Quicksoil
- Heavily reworked Strongholds!

[ BALANCE ]
- Nerfed damage of several dragon attacks
- Nerfed protection of Copper Chestplate & Leggings; 5 & 4 -> 4 & 3
- Slightly buffed Copper Armor's mining speed boost; 0.5 -> 0.55
- Anvils now repair items 100% with only one required material
- Golden Dart Shooters can now be crafted with regular planks, no longer gating them behind the Aether
- DRASTICALLY reduced spawn rate of Zephyrs
- Buffed loot of Aether boss chests
- Aether flawless-exclusive boss drop system is no more; instead, an extra drop is rewarded as the flawless reward
- Candy can now stack to 16 and heals one heart (But still has the Nausea debuff after eating too much)
- All Cookies now heal a heart and a half
- All Sweet Foods are now always edible and grant a small speed boost upon consumption
- Nerfed animal spawn cap; 25 -> 20

- Aether Treasure Chests and Chest Mimics now have proper fullblock models (courtesy of Quadruplay)
- Attack Speed no longer shows up as an attribute in tooltips (courtesy of Quadruplay)
