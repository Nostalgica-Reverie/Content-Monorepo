# Vital 26.07.2
Vital will no longer be directly advertising itself as an Essential Mod alternative, and instead a QoL modpack with all of Essential's features; and more!

Vital was initially made out of frustration towards the Essential Mod's monetization practices and its closed source nature in an otherwise overwhelmingly Free and Open Source community. However, Vital has not sufficiently acted as a proper alternative due to limitations with being a modpack, and we want to take the pack forward in features while keeping all of its current QoL features (which just so happens to include a ton of Essential features!).

## What does this mean for Vital?
Not much, to be perfectly honest! Since we acquired and revived the project, Vital has already been acting more and more as a QoL/Performance modpack than just "the essential alternative" modpack. It has gained features like build in recording, music management, among an increasing QoL set of mods we have added that simply just have nothing to do with replacing Essential. We want to make this modpack good and innovative, but I do not think its original purpose fits its current goals

## Breaking away from Additive
Firstly, this is, just as in Re-Console's case, not a jab at Additive or Devin in any way! I would consider Devin one of my biggest inspirations in the community, and he has helped me out so much across my time in the community that without him - Reverie wouldn't have existed at all. I still plan to work with Devin over at SkywardMC! However, it is simply easier to maintain a pack built off of Simply Optimized Forked (our dedicated performance stack, which happens to be published on platforms so others may use it) than it is Additive as we have control over curation, configs, etc.
