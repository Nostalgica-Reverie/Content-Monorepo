- Added two new Game Drops: the Mounts of Mayhem and Tiny Takeover!

Mounts of Mayhem:
- Added the Nautilus, an mount that grants the ability to float, dash and breathe underwater!
- Added the Zombie Nautilus, an undead underwater mount for the Drowned
- Added the Camel Husk and Parched, which are Husk-like variants of the Camel and Skeleton
- The Zombie Horse can now spawn naturally!
- Added the Spear, a new tiered weapon with two new attack types
  - The Jab attack can be used to quickly hit enemies, with a cooldown for each material
  - The Charge attack can be scaled with velocity, so hitting faster means more damage!
- Added the Lunge enchantment for Spears
- Added Nautilus Armor, with material variants for all metal types
- Added Netherite Horse Armor, obtained by upgrading existing Diamond Horse Armor in a Smithing Table
- Horses can now swim in water, rather than sinking!

Tiny Takeover:
- Overhauled all baby mobs, with newer, smaller, cuter models!
- Added the Golden Dandelion, which is a golden dandelion that can stop aging when used on mobs!
- Added new sounds for kittens, chicks, foal, piglets and wolf pups!
- Added new sound variants for adult mobs:
  - Cats: Royal and Classic
  - Pigs: Mini, Big and Classic
  - Cows: Moody and Classic
  - Chickens: Picky and Classic
  - Classic includes all the old sounds
- Rabbits have a new look, with an updated model and texture
- Name Tags can now be crafted with one iron nugget and one paper!
- Added a new Trumpet instrument to the Note Block
- Stone and Deepslate can be cobbled through a Stonecutter
  - Deepslate can also now be crafted into its variants
